# Privacy and ethics

This project concerns children and potential learning difficulties. Collection
or research use requires the institution's ethics approval, informed consent and
assent procedures, a defined lawful basis, and review by qualified supervisors.

## Required safeguards

- Identify children only with pseudonymous IDs in application data.
- Minimise collection and keep camera processing on the edge where possible.
- Never commit raw video, identifying records, consent forms, medical records,
  credentials, private datasets, or model artifacts.
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest and restrict access by role.
- Define retention, deletion, incident response, and data-subject procedures
  before participant data is collected.
- Audit access and model/profile generation without logging sensitive payloads.
- Assess model performance, calibration, bias, and failure modes across relevant
  participant groups before use.
- Provide teachers with uncertainty, important features, model versions, and
  appropriate next actions—not opaque labels alone.

Synthetic examples must be clearly labelled. Pseudonymisation reduces risk but
does not make sensitive child data anonymous.
