# Educational screening boundary

The system supports early educational screening and classroom decision-making.
It does **not** diagnose dyscalculia, prescribe treatment, or replace assessment
by qualified educational, psychological, or clinical professionals.

Low, moderate, and high indicators express patterns observed by a validated
research method with uncertainty. They are not clinical categories. Thresholds
must be configurable, versioned, documented, and labelled unvalidated until the
research validation process is complete.

Teacher-facing outputs should include:

- the evidence and time window used;
- data-quality and confidence limitations;
- important contributing features in plain language;
- the model/method version;
- a proportionate educational next action; and
- the non-diagnostic disclaimer.

Insufficient visibility, missing history, distribution shift, or low confidence
must produce an uncertain/insufficient result or escalation for review—not a
fabricated confident risk label.
